FORMULA

Tₗ = Tₛ exp(μₛ θ)

Assumptions

Required: a flexible rope with negligible mass and bending stiffness, uniform static friction, and continuous drum contact. θ is the total contact wrap angle and may exceed one revolution. The result is a friction limit. Excluded: rope breaking strength, drum strength, added distributed loads, and groove effects.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
